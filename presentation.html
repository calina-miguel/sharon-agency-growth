Acquisition System

Two Lead Streams With One Approval Layer

The system gives the agency two separate growth engines, then routes both into one controlled operating workflow.

  • Insurance buyers and agent candidates use separate pages, messages, and follow-up paths
  • Lead sources include Meta, Google Search, LinkedIn, organic content, and referral campaigns
  • Social Hub generates posts, queues content, tracks engagement, and supports recurring lead scans
  • Intake, approval, scheduling, and reporting keep the team in control before outreach happens
  • Compliance Watch and AEO Studio support safer acquisition copy and better search visibility

Dashboard

Lead Acquisition Command Center

The command center gives the team a fast read on acquisition activity before they open any detailed workspace.

  • Buyer and recruiting lead cards show volume by stream
  • Approval and approved cards show what needs attention now
  • Each card opens the area where those leads are stored
  • Stream buttons scan buyer or agent interest and preload intake with realistic source signals

Lead Sources

Real Channels Feeding The System

This section explains where leads come from in a real deployment and how those channels feed the system.

  • Buyer demand comes from Meta ads, Google Search Ads, local education content, and referral links
  • Recruiting demand comes from LinkedIn, career search ads, recruiting posts, and short-form content
  • The system treats clicks, form fills, replies, and comments as acquisition signals
  • Matched activity routes into intake, approval, scheduling, and reporting

Scheduling

Calendar, Notes, And Outcomes

The schedule view turns approved leads into accountable follow-up so good prospects do not sit idle.

  • Calendar-style cards show upcoming buyer callbacks and recruiting conversations
  • Date, time, notes, and outcome fields keep context attached to each lead
  • Status choices move leads toward callback, consultation, interview, follow-up, or closeout
  • The Approved metric opens this workspace directly

Lead Manager

Stored Leads Split By Buyer And Recruit Type

The lead manager keeps buyer opportunities and recruiting candidates separate, while giving both streams the same operating discipline.

  • Customers / Clients tab stores consumer leads and buyer follow-up stages
  • Sales Agents tab stores candidates and recruiting stages
  • Each tab includes status, flow, follow-up actions, links, reporting, and content plan
  • Approved leads move through pipeline stages instead of staying in a flat list

Insurance Customers

Dedicated Customer Acquisition Page

This public-facing page gives buyer campaigns a focused destination and keeps consumer intent separate from recruiting intent.

  • Messaging speaks to families, homeowners, and business owners
  • The request form captures name, concern, and the buyer’s own words
  • Submissions feed guided intake, lead scoring, and human approval
  • The page can receive traffic from ads, content links, search, and referrals

Sales Agent Recruiting

Dedicated Recruitment Page

This page supports the agency’s second priority: finding strong sales agent candidates without mixing recruiting copy into consumer campaigns.

  • Recruiting copy covers licensing, mentorship, agency support, and sales opportunity fit
  • The form captures experience level and candidate motivation
  • Submissions route into recruiting intake and approval
  • Qualified candidates move toward interview or licensing review

Social Hub

Connected Account Stats, Publishing, And Lead Scans

The Social Hub is the operating area for social acquisition: enrolled accounts, performance stats, generated posts, publishing actions, and lead-scan readiness.

  • Connected accounts show views, engagements, clicks, and attributed leads by platform
  • Content Studio generates buyer or recruiting posts from a goal, topic, and call to action
  • The user can publish to selected enrolled accounts or queue the posts for review
  • The publishing log records what went out and where it went
  • If no accounts are connected, the app explains why enrollment is required before scans or publishing

Lead Intake

Signals Become Reviewable Lead Records

The intake view turns campaign activity into a structured lead record before anyone follows up.

  • The user chooses Customer or Sales Agent to match the source of interest
  • The message area shows the captured signal, such as an ad click, page visit, comment, or form response
  • Running intake produces a fit score, summary, recommended next step, and outreach draft
  • The lead moves to the approval queue instead of sending an automatic message immediately

Compliance Watch

Current Guidance Before Campaign Changes

Compliance Watch gives the team a review step before changing ad copy, scripts, lead capture forms, or outreach flows.

  • The source watchlist includes insurance advertising, social media, lead generation, licensing, and telemarketing guidance
  • Recommendations focus on consent, separate buyer and recruiting intent, and avoiding misleading claims
  • Source cards keep the team close to the external guidance behind the review
  • The system keeps public claims and automated outreach behind human approval

AEO Studio

Search Questions Become Lead Paths

AEO Studio helps the agency answer the questions buyers and potential agents already ask online, then routes that interest to the right page.

  • Buyer questions become educational content and customer-page calls to action
  • Recruiting questions become career content and recruitment-page calls to action
  • Each answer snippet stays plain-language and avoids product advice or guarantees
  • Compliance Watch supports review before publishing public-facing answers

Human Approval

Approval Before Automation Acts

The approval queue gives the team a pause point before outreach, booking, or recruiting follow-up happens.

  • Each card shows lead type, fit score, summary, draft copy, and recommended next step
  • The team can approve good opportunities or reject poor-fit leads
  • Approved buyer leads move toward callback or consultation
  • Approved recruiting leads move toward interview or licensing review

Campaign Builder

Shareable Links And Outreach Copy

The campaign builder creates the basic link and message package a team can use for ads, posts, referral partners, or email outreach.

  • The user chooses the lead stream, audience, and offer
  • The app generates a shareable campaign URL with stream and audience context
  • The outreach copy explains the offer and tells the team how intake will handle responses
  • Buyer campaigns and recruiting campaigns stay separated from the first click

Interface Updates

Quick Start Guide And Daily Controls

The interface includes a dedicated Quick Start Guide page that opens real app areas and highlights the exact controls users should review.

  • Quick Start Guide cards jump to actual app sections and highlight live controls in place
  • Profile menu opens from the profile avatar or name and supports display-name and portrait changes
  • Dark mode, light mode, and reduced motion settings support different presentation environments
  • The sidebar is resizable on desktop and collapses into a mobile menu

Reporting

Lead Acquisition Scorecard

The reporting view gives the team a simple scorecard for acquisition volume and movement through the workflow.

  • Total captured shows buyer and recruiting activity together
  • Average fit score gives a quick quality signal across current leads
  • Pending and approved counts show workload and follow-up readiness
  • The funnel view explains how leads move from capture to approval or rejection
